# Billable Units

Polyaxon Cloud [pricing](/pricing) is based on your plan and the capacity assigned to your organization for each monthly billing period.

- **Seats**: The number and type of users who can access your organization, including developer, read-only, and billing seats.
- **Clusters**: The number of agents connecting Kubernetes clusters or namespaces to the Polyaxon control plane. Additional agents add multi-cluster or multi-namespace capacity.
- **Concurrency**: The maximum number of runs that can execute at the same time across your connected clusters and queues.
- **Queues and schedules**: The number of queues and active schedules available to your organization.

Each plan includes a base capacity. You can use our [pricing calculator](/pricing?calculatorOpen=true) to estimate the monthly cost of additional concurrency, queues, schedules, or clusters.

## FAQ

**How can I track my Polyaxon Cloud usage?** Organization owners and admins can monitor usage from the organization billing page.

**What counts as a seat?** A seat grants a user access to an organization. Developer, read-only, and billing seats have different permissions and pricing.

**How is concurrency measured?** Concurrency is a capacity limit: it controls how many runs can execute simultaneously. It is not based on compute hours, CPU usage, or GPU usage.

**How are clusters counted?** Polyaxon Agent connects a Kubernetes namespace or cluster to the Cloud control plane. Plans include a defined number of agents, and additional agents provide capacity for more clusters or namespaces.
