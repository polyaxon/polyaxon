# How much will Polyaxon cost?

Platform and Teams plans are available as monthly subscriptions. Enterprise offers custom pricing.

Your aggregate plan cost will be available in the billing page under the organization's settings.

All plans are priced per seat, with a minimum number of seats per organization. Platform includes 3 developer seats, Teams includes 6 developer seats, while Enterprise offers a custom number of seats in your organization.

Each of our plans comes with a defined usage quota, you can scale your plan with additional concurrency and/or compute clusters.

For large numbers of users and custom pricing, support options, full on-premise deployment, or to discuss training and services options, please contact us.
