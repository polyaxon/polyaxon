# How to invite co-workers to your Polyaxon Organization

## How do I invite someone to a Polyaxon Organization?

Open **Organization → Settings → Members** and invite a new member using the email address they use in Polyaxon. Organization owners and managers can invite members and assign roles. For details, see the [organization membership documentation](/docs/administration/organizations/members/).

## I cannot join a Polyaxon Organization

### Are you using the correct email address?

If you have been invited to an organization, make sure you log in with the exact email address that received the invitation. If you log in with SSO (e.g., GitHub or Google), your email address might be different from what you expected.

Open your user profile to check the email address associated with your account.

### Who can resend my invitation?

Ask an organization owner or manager to resend your invitation.

## Access Roles in Polyaxon

### What types of access can I grant to data in Polyaxon?

Organization roles include Owner, Billing, Manager, Admin, Member, Viewer, and Outsider. You can see the permissions granted to each role in the [role-based access control documentation](/docs/administration/rbac/).
