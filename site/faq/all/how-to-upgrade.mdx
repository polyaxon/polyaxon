# How do I upgrade my Polyaxon account?

You can upgrade your Polyaxon organization by navigating to **Organization → Settings → Billing**.

You can find out more about the Polyaxon Platform and Teams plans on our [pricing](/pricing) page and in the [Enterprise Edition documentation](/docs/editions/enterprise).

## What payment methods do you support?

We currently support credit card payments via Stripe.

## How can I change my plan / payment method?

Open **Organization → Settings → Billing** to manage your plan or payment method. See the [billing documentation](/docs/administration/organizations/billing/) for details.
